Case study

Messenger AI Chatbot

A custom Odoo 19 module that connects a business's Facebook Messenger page to an AI chatbot. The chatbot replies to customer messages automatically, based on the business information configured in Odoo.

Odoo

personMy Role

I designed and built the module: the Odoo 19 custom module in Python, the Meta Messenger integration and the AI integration.

reportThe Problem

Small businesses receive the same customer questions on Messenger all day. Answering them by hand is slow, and a generic bot does not know the business.

lightbulbThe Solution

The business keeps its details in Odoo. The module reads that information and uses it to let an AI chatbot answer customer messages on the Messenger page automatically.

codeTech Stack

  • Platform: Odoo 19
  • Language: Python
  • Integrations: Meta Messenger, AI integration

starKey Features

  • Connects a Facebook Messenger page to Odoo
  • Automatic AI replies to customer messages
  • Answers are based on the business information configured in Odoo
